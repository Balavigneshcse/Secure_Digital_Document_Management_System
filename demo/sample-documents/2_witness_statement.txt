STATEMENT OF WITNESS
Recorded under Section 161 CrPC

Case: CR-2026-KRR1-0002 (Cattle theft, Konalampatti)
Recorded by: SI Officer, Karur Town Police Station
Date: 15/09/2026

I, Mrs. Pushpalatha M, aged 41 years, resident of Konalampatti village, Karur district, do hereby state as
follows:

On the night of 14/09/2026, at around 2:00 AM, I woke up on hearing the cattle shed gate creak. Looking out of my
window I saw two men, one of medium build wearing a checked shirt and the other taller wearing a dark jacket,
leading two cows out through the side path towards the canal road. I did not recognise either of them clearly in
the dark and did not raise an alarm out of fear. In the morning, on informing my neighbour Mr. Muthu Kumar, we
found the shed gate broken open and two cows missing. I am willing to identify the shorter man if shown to me
again, as I noticed he was limping slightly on his left leg.

This statement is true to the best of my knowledge.
