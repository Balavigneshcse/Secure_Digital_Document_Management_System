FORENSIC SCIENCE LABORATORY REPORT

Lab: Regional Forensic Science Laboratory, Chennai
Report No. FSL/2026/0341                   Date: 21/09/2026
Case reference: CR-2026-CHN2-0005 (Counterfeit goods seizure)
Examination requested by: Chennai North Police Station

Exhibits received: 14 branded electronic items (earphones and chargers) seized from a wholesale godown on
16/09/2026, sealed in exhibit bags marked A1 to A14.

Examination:
The packaging, holograms and print quality of all 14 exhibits were compared against genuine samples obtained from
the authorised distributor. Microscopic examination of the holographic security labels revealed inconsistent
refraction patterns on 11 of the 14 items (A1-A6, A9-A13), indicating these are counterfeit reproductions. The
remaining 3 items (A7, A8, A14) could not be conclusively distinguished from genuine stock using the available
reference samples and require manufacturer verification.

Opinion: 11 of the 14 examined exhibits (A1-A6, A9-A13) are counterfeit. Findings on the remaining 3 exhibits are
inconclusive.

Examined by: Scientific Officer, Regional Forensic Science Laboratory, Chennai
