CHARGE SHEET / FINAL REPORT
Under Section 173 CrPC

Case: CR-2026-CHN1-0002 (Chain snatching, T Nagar)
FIR No. FIR-0502/2026, Chennai Central Police Station
Court: Judicial Magistrate Court, Chennai Central

1. Name of the investigating officer: SI Officer, Chennai Central Police Station
2. Date of occurrence: 10/09/2026
3. Date charge sheet filed: 20/09/2026

Facts of the case:
The complainant, Mrs. Revathi N, was walking along Usman Road, T Nagar when two persons on a motorcycle snatched
her gold chain weighing approximately 22 grams, valued at Rs. 1,85,000, and fled towards Pondy Bazaar. Based on
CCTV footage obtained from three shops along the route and cell tower analysis, the accused, Mr. Karthik S and
Mr. Ajay Kumar R, were identified and apprehended on 18/09/2026. The stolen chain was recovered from a pawn shop
where it had been pledged.

Sections of law applied: Section 304 BNS (snatching), Section 309(4) BNS (robbery), Section 3(5) BNS (common
intention)

List of witnesses: Mrs. Revathi N (complainant), shop owner - Ganesh Stores (CCTV), pawn shop owner

Opinion: Sufficient evidence exists to place the accused on trial. The case is forwarded to the Judicial
Magistrate Court, Chennai Central, for further proceedings.
