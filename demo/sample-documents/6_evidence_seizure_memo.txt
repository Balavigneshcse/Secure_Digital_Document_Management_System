SEIZURE MEMO (Mahazar)
Under Section 47 BNSS

Case: CR-2026-KRR2-0004 (Counterfeit currency recovery)
Police Station: Karur Rural
Date and place of seizure: 16/09/2026, Main Road Provision Stall, Aravakurichi

In the presence of the undersigned witnesses, the following articles were seized from the possession of the shop
owner during a routine inspection:

1. Counterfeit currency notes of Rs. 500 denomination - 14 pieces, total face value Rs. 7,000
2. One plastic pouch in which the notes were found concealed

The seized articles were sealed in a cloth bundle, labelled Exhibit MO-1, and the seal impression was affixed in
the presence of the witnesses named below. The shop owner was informed of the seizure and a copy of this memo was
handed to him.

Witnesses:
1. Ravi Shankar, resident of Aravakurichi
2. Elumalai P, resident of Aravakurichi

Seizing officer: SI Officer, Karur Rural Police Station
Sections of law: Section 178 BNS (possession of forged currency), Section 180 BNS
