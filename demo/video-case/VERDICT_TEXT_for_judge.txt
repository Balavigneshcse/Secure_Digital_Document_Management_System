Verdict: Convicted
Sentence / order: 3 years rigorous imprisonment and fine of Rs. 10,000 under Section 304(2) BNS

Reasons for the judgment:
The prosecution has proved the offence of snatching beyond reasonable doubt. The complainant identified the chain
recovered from the accused as her own, supported by the purchase invoice and the forensic report on weight, purity
and hallmark. The eyewitness, Mr. Murugesan K, consistently described the pillion rider and the motorcycle whose
registration ended in 21, and the enhanced CCTV footage independently confirms this. The accused offered no
credible explanation for possession of the chain three days after the incident. The chain is to be returned to the
complainant.
