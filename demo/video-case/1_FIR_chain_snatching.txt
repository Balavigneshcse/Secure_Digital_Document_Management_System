2. Details of the offence:
On 22/09/2026 at about 18:55 hours, the complainant was walking home from the Karur Bus Stand along Kovai Road
when two men on a black motorcycle approached her from behind. The pillion rider snatched her gold chain
(6 sovereigns, approximately 48 grams, valued at Rs. 4,80,000) from her neck and the motorcycle sped away towards
Thanthonimalai. The complainant sustained a scratch injury on her neck and fell to the ground. Passers-by helped her
and called the police control room at 19:05 hours.

3. Accused (if known): Two unknown men. The motorcycle was black, the last two digits of the registration
number were noted by a witness as "21".

4. Sections of law: Section 304(2) BNS (snatching), Section 3(5) BNS (common intention)

5. Place of occurrence: Kovai Road, opposite the Karur Bus Stand, Karur

6. Witnesses: Mr. Murugesan K (tea stall owner, Kovai Road)

Action taken: Case registered and investigation taken up by the Sub-Inspector, Karur Town Police Station. Scene of
occurrence inspected; CCTV footage of nearby shops requisitioned.
