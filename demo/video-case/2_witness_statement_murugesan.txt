Name of witness: Mr. Murugesan K
Age: 41 years
Occupation: Tea stall owner
Address: No. 3, Kovai Road, Karur

Statement:
I run a tea stall on Kovai Road opposite the Karur Bus Stand. On 22/09/2026 at about 18:55 hours I saw an elderly
woman, later known to me as Mrs. Lakshmi Devi, walking along the road. A black motorcycle with two men came from
behind. The man sitting at the back, wearing a grey shirt and a black cap, pulled the chain from her neck. She
screamed and fell down. The motorcycle turned towards Thanthonimalai at high speed. I noticed that the registration
number ended with "21". I ran to help the lady and telephoned the police control room.

I can identify the man in the grey shirt if I see him again. I have read the statement and it is true and correct.

Signature of witness: Murugesan K
