FORENSIC SCIENCE LABORATORY REPORT

Lab: District Forensic Science Laboratory, Karur
Report No. FSL/KRR/2026/0198               Date: 26/09/2026
Case reference: FIR No. 0688/2026, Karur Town Police Station (Chain snatching)
Examination requested by: Sub-Inspector, Karur Town Police Station

Exhibits received:
Exhibit A: One gold chain (broken), sealed in packet A, recovered on 25/09/2026 from the accused.
Exhibit B: CCTV footage (two clips, 18:53 to 18:58 hours on 22/09/2026) from a jewellery shop on Kovai Road, on a
sealed pen drive.

Examination:
Exhibit A: The chain weighs 47.9 grams, gold purity 22 carat by XRF analysis. The broken link and the clasp show
fresh tool-free tear marks consistent with a forceful pull. The design, weight and hallmark match the purchase
invoice produced by the complainant.
Exhibit B: The enhanced footage shows a black motorcycle with the registration number ending in "21" and a pillion
rider in a grey shirt and black cap pulling an object from the neck of a pedestrian. The rider of the motorcycle
matches the second accused in build and clothing. The footage was not edited; the hash of the pen drive contents
was recorded at the time of receipt.

Opinion: Exhibit A is the gold chain described in the complaint. Exhibit B shows the incident and the motorcycle
used by the accused.

Examined by: Scientific Officer, District Forensic Science Laboratory, Karur
