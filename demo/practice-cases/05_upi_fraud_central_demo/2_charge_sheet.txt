FINAL REPORT / CHARGE SHEET
Under Section 193 BNSS (formerly Section 173 CrPC)

Case: FIR No. 0288/2026, Central Police Station (Demo)
Date of filing: 29/09/2026

1. Name of the Investigating Officer: Insp. Officer One (Demo)

2. Brief facts of the case:
Investigation established that the four unauthorised UPI transactions were routed to a mule bank account opened
in Demo City using fabricated documents. Call detail records traced the calling number to a SIM card obtained on a
forged identity. Bank statements of the mule account show the funds were withdrawn through ATMs within two hours
of the fraud.

3. Evidence collected:
- Complainant's bank statement showing the four unauthorised debits.
- Call detail records for the calling number, for the period 18/09/2026.
- Mule account opening documents obtained from the bank under Section 94 BNSS.
- CCTV footage of the ATM withdrawals.

4. Sections of law: Section 318(4) BNS, Section 66C and 66D of the Information Technology Act, 2000

5. Opinion: Sufficient evidence exists to place the case for trial. Investigation into the identity of the
individual who opened the mule account is continuing.

Submitted by: Insp. Officer One (Demo), Central Police Station (Demo)
