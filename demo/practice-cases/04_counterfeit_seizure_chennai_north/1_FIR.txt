Under Section 173 BNSS (formerly Section 154 CrPC)

FIR No. 0933/2026                          Police Station: Chennai North
District: Chennai                          State: Tamil Nadu
Date and time of FIR: 16/09/2026, 15:30 hours

1. Complainant / Informant:
Name: Inspector Raghavan, Chennai North Police Station (on a specific complaint from the brand's authorised
distributor)

2. Details of the offence:
On 16/09/2026 at about 14:00 hours, on receiving credible information, a police team raided a wholesale shop on
Ritchie Street run by Mr. Suresh Babu and recovered 210 units of branded earphones and chargers suspected to be
counterfeit, packed for sale to retailers. The packaging closely imitated the genuine brand but the holograms
appeared inconsistent on visual inspection.

3. Accused: Mr. Suresh Babu, proprietor, Ritchie Street, Chennai

4. Sections of law: Section 105 of the Trade Marks Act 1999, Section 318(4) BNS (cheating)

5. Place of occurrence: Wholesale shop, Ritchie Street, Chennai

Action taken: 210 units seized and sealed; samples sent for forensic examination; accused questioned and
released on station bail pending investigation.
