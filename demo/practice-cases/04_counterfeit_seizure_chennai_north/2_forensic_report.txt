FORENSIC SCIENCE LABORATORY REPORT

Lab: Regional Forensic Science Laboratory, Chennai
Report No. FSL/2026/0402                   Date: 22/09/2026
Case reference: FIR No. 0933/2026, Chennai North Police Station (Counterfeit goods seizure)
Examination requested by: Chennai North Police Station

Exhibits received: 12 sample units (6 earphones, 6 chargers) drawn from the 210 units seized on 16/09/2026,
sealed in exhibit bags marked A1 to A12.

Examination:
The packaging, holograms and print quality of all 12 exhibits were compared against genuine samples obtained from
the authorised distributor. Microscopic examination of the holographic security labels revealed inconsistent
refraction patterns on 9 of the 12 exhibits (A1-A5, A9-A12), indicating these are counterfeit reproductions. The
remaining 3 items (A6, A7, A8) could not be conclusively distinguished from genuine stock using the available
reference samples and require manufacturer verification.

Opinion: 9 of the 12 examined exhibits are counterfeit. Findings on the remaining 3 exhibits are inconclusive.

Examined by: Scientific Officer, Regional Forensic Science Laboratory, Chennai
